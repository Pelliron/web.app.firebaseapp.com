Pelliron Universal LLC company profile

Pelliron Universal LLC is a global brokerage established by specialists with experience in trading, investment and brokerage.

Background and expertise

Pelliron Universal LLC grew out of several successful research and investment initiatives. Its founders are traders and brokers from different investment fields bringing together hands-on experience and a clear understanding of client needs.

Client-centred service

Pelliron builds its service around the trader. The company prioritises comfort, security, fast order execution and straightforward communication with its specialists.

Available markets and instruments

Pelliron connects clients with forex, stock and commodity markets. Clients can also access tools for investment planning, hedging and private capital management.

Platform access

Pelliron Trading Platform can be used in a web browser, on iOS and Android devices, Windows and macOS computers, and tablets.

Service reliability and support

The company prioritises high-quality order execution, client fund security and round-the-clock support. Pelliron specialists help resolve organisational and technical issues.

Company objectives

Pelliron aims to provide practical conditions for comfortable trading on international financial markets and bring the essential tools for traders together in one service.
