Privacy Notice

Updated on 22 July 2026. This notice outlines how personal information is handled when people use the Pelliron website.

1. General principles2. Data categories3. Reasons for processing4. Legal grounds5. Retention and security6. External recipients7. Your rights8. Third-party links and updates

1. General principles

This notice sets out how information is handled when visitors use the Pelliron website or its available contact options.

2. Data categories

The hosting provider may automatically log an IP address, browser type, request time, requested URL and server response code. The contact form prepares a draft email in the user’s email application and does not submit data to the website server.

3. Reasons for processing

Technical information may be applied for security, error diagnosis, abuse prevention and website availability. This information is not used for automated financial decision-making.

4. Legal grounds

Basic technical logs may be handled on the basis of a legitimate interest in website security and stability. The user initiates any email communication.

5. Retention and security

Hosting settings determine how long logs are retained. Appropriate safeguards should be used, although sending data online always carries some risk.

6. External recipients

The hosting provider may process the data and the user’s email service. The website does not sell users’ personal information or automatically share it with the broker.

7. Your rights

Where applicable law allows, users can ask to access, correct, erase or restrict the handling of their data. Requests should be sent to the email on the contact page.

8. Third-party links and updates

Third-party resources follow their own privacy rules. This notice can be revised when site functionality or legal obligations change.
